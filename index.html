Cross-Disciplinary Recruitment Findings

  • Technical & Master's Premium: M.Tech, MCA, and B.Tech candidates command the highest average salary packages (up to ₹9.08L).
  • Work Experience & Internships are Decisive: Industry experience increases recruitment probability by +49.9 percentage points across all streams.
  • Academic Consistency: Recruiters prioritize candidates with consistent 10th (`ssc_p`) and 12th (`hsc_p`) performance along with aptitude scores (`etest_p`).
