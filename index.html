Key Academic Stage Scores (Placed vs Unplaced)

Insight: 10th (`ssc_p`) and 12th (`hsc_p`) scores show a massive +14.2% and +11.5% gap between placed vs unplaced candidates, whereas MBA score (`mba_p`) shows only a +1.0% difference.

Placement Rate by Prior Work Experience

Insight: Prior work experience boosts placement rates from 59.57% to 86.49% (+26.9 percentage points higher probability).

Placement Rate by MBA Specialisation

Insight: Marketing & Finance candidates achieved a 79.17% placement rate vs 55.79% for Marketing & HR (+23.4% placement rate advantage).

Salary Offer Distribution (Placed Students)

Top 3 Recruitment Key Findings

  • Work Experience is Paramount: Having prior work experience increases recruitment odds by +26.9 percentage points (86.5% vs 59.6%).
  • Consistent High School Performance Trumps MBA Scores: Recruiters prioritize candidates with strong secondary education history over MBA GPA alone.
  • Specialisation Premium: Marketing & Finance delivers higher placement rates (79.2% vs 55.8%) and higher average packages (₹2.98L vs ₹2.70L).
